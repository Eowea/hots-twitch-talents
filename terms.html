Talents shows, during a live Heroes of the Storm match, the talents chosen by all ten players. It has two parts: the extension itself, displayed on Twitch, and a reader program the broadcaster runs on their own computer.

Keep that code to yourself. Whoever holds it can publish a panel on your channel — nothing else, but that is already too much.
